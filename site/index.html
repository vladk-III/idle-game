How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, choose Install anyway. That's normal for apps that don't come from the Play Store.

To update, download and install again. Your railway and focus tokens are kept.

Inside the game

What it's like

Build a railway

Connect towns, farms and factories. Trains run on their own and towns grow.

Focus mode

Ride along trackside, in a carriage or in the cab. Tap, hold and swipe as a quiet fidget.

Earn by focusing

Every 5 minutes in Focus mode earns a token for permanent upgrades.

Seasons and eras

Snowy winters and golden autumns, from steam engines to maglev.
